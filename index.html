About

Engineering-Focused, Fabrication-Aware

Unity Structural Solution is a premium structural engineering consultancy built for the construction industry.

We Don't Just Produce Drawings.

We understand how structures are engineered, detailed, fabricated, and constructed. Every project we work on is approached with engineering integrity, fabrication practicality, and a focus on delivering value.

With 22+ years of combined experience, we serve contractors, fabricators, developers, and international engineering firms across the USA, Australia, Europe, the UAE, and beyond.

  • Engineering-focused + fabrication-aware
  • Direct access to senior engineers
  • International standards compliance
  • Quality-controlled deliverables

How We Approach Engineering

We start with the end in mind — how will this structure be fabricated, transported, and erected? This practical perspective informs every design and detailing decision we make.

Our lean, senior-led structure means you work directly with the engineers who design and detail your project — no account managers, no layers of communication. Just clear, professional engineering.

International Codes: AISC 360, Eurocode, ACI 318, BNBC • Software: Tekla Structures, STAAD.Pro, ETABS, AutoCAD
