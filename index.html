About

Engineering Solutions Designed Around Real Construction

Structural Engineering. Built for the Way You Build.

Unity Structural Solution provides structural analysis, design, BIM modeling, steel detailing, and engineering documentation for projects across the globe. We work with structural engineering companies, steel fabricators, PEB manufacturers, contractors, architects, and developers.

Our approach is straightforward: understand the project requirements, apply the appropriate engineering standards, and deliver clear, coordinated documentation that supports fabrication and construction.

  • Engineering analysis and design
  • Tekla modeling and steel detailing
  • Fabrication-ready shop drawings
  • Remote collaboration for international projects
Connect with us on LinkedIn

Remote Engineering Support. Worldwide.

We collaborate remotely with clients across time zones. Our workflow is built for international project delivery — clear communication, coordinated deliverables, and professional engineering documentation.

Design Standards: AISC 360, Eurocode, ACI 318, BNBC (applied according to project requirements and jurisdiction).

Software: STAAD.Pro (analysis & design), ETABS (RCC design), Tekla Structures (BIM & detailing), AutoCAD.

We don't just produce drawings. We deliver engineering that supports fabrication, construction, and project success.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide structural steel detailing?

Yes — we provide Tekla steel detailing, shop drawings, GA drawings, and fabrication documentation.

Can you work with international projects?

Yes — we collaborate remotely with clients worldwide, providing engineering support across time zones.

What drawings and information do you need to start?
