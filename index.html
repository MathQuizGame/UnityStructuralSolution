FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What PEB services do you provide?

PEB tender and proposal engineering, structural analysis, estimation, BOQ, MTO, Tekla detailing and AutoCAD documentation.

Can you prepare PEB proposal estimates?

Yes — we provide complete PEB estimation including primary steel, secondary steel, purlins, girts, decking, sheeting and trims.

Can you provide complete MTO / BOQ?

Yes — we prepare detailed MTO and BOQ with weight schedules and quantity verification.

Do you provide Tekla detailing?

Yes — our senior detailing specialist provides Tekla 3D modeling, fabrication drawings, erection drawings and BOM.

Can you work as an extension of an existing engineering team?

Yes — we provide remote engineering support designed to complement and extend your existing team.

Can you review an existing STAAD.Pro model?

Yes — we can review models, verify loads, check member designs and provide feedback.

Can you support only part of a project?

Yes — we can provide support for specific phases including analysis, estimation, detailing or documentation.

What drawings / information do you need to start?

Project requirements, design criteria, architectural drawings and any available engineering data.

Can you work remotely with international clients?

Yes — we collaborate remotely across time zones with coordinated file exchange and clear communication.

Which software do you use?

STAAD.Pro, ETABS, Tekla Structures, AutoCAD, Excel and Microsoft Office.

Which standards can you work with?

AISC 360, ACI 318, BNBC, Eurocode and project-specific requirements.

Can you handle confidential project information?

Project information is handled professionally and used for project evaluation, communication and delivery. Formal NDA arrangements can be discussed for projects requiring contractual confidentiality.
